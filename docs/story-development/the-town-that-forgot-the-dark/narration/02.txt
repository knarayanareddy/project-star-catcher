The first suspension lasted two nights. A storm had flooded the lower pumps. People would have died without power. Harl kept the original order where everyone could see it. The extensions were underneath. A good reason is a useful place to hide a bad habit. It continues looking brave long after the emergency has changed its address.

Teo had written a shutdown plan. Feeding first. Collector second. Return cue. Shutters. One complete hour. The backup cells could carry the clinic and pumps for seventy-five minutes, not the factories and decorative towers as well. This was not a choice between light and death. It was a choice about what we were willing to stop doing.

Rest. Rise. Return. Three little words I had made soft enough for children. To the moths they meant gather, feed, and go home. But home was still lit. So they circled back to the sleepers. My voice was not comforting a town. It was operating a shift that no longer had an end.

Under the hood, one moth stopped moving. I lifted the edge to make sure it was all right. It startled, and I put the darkness back. I have reassured thousands of people by checking on them. There are occasions when checking is simply another way of refusing to let a thing rest.

Teo had signed for one shift. The extensions carried the keeper's mark. Beside them lay my retirement form: permission to run my recorded voice forever. They were offering to let me rest by making sure the part of me they needed never could. I left the form unsigned.
