## What “complete screenplay” means here

Every episode has a beginning-to-end script: scene headings, playable action, full dialogue, transitions, and an ending. There are no “dialogue to come,” scene-summary substitutes, or unwritten middle sections. These are literary first drafts, not locked production pages. Fountain can be imported into compatible screenplay software for pagination and PDF export.

Scene-duration notes are editorial targets. They include pauses and nonverbal action, not merely speech. Each episode allocates **9:45 story + 0:15 end credits = 10:00**. A timed table read and animatic must validate actual runtime; this package does not claim an exact runtime from word count or page count alone.

## Validation

Run `python validate_package.py` from this folder (or `python docs/story-development/good-mourning/validate_package.py` from the repository root). It checks all 42 scene IDs and matching treatment/script time ranges, credits allocations, package links, and the bridge arithmetic. It does not validate performed runtime or substitute for editorial, clinical, or legal review.

## Important canon clarifications

- **Rena is dead. She has no current Hold treatment.** Mara's canceled appointments concern Mara's release from the Hold for Rena's death. Rena's surviving account is only an archive/message service. The older outline's references to “Rena's releases” are corrected accordingly.
- Isha is being asked to renew **her household certificate**, not treatment for a dead person. Group certification, individual clinical consent, and archival-message subscriptions are separate products that WellSpring improperly bundles.
- The opening seven-day clock is the annual **building contract**. Individual Hold renewals follow their own monthly schedules.
- **Escrow money is not secretly spent.** A voted cooperative reserve pays the ten-day housing bridge. Disputed administration fees remain sealed in escrow. Clinical fees continue to be paid. The hearing orders future contract-funded administration savings toward transition care and restores the separately funded housing supplement.
- The final victory is an interim, local order plus paid care arrangements—not national abolition of WellSpring or a cure for grief.

**Creative boundary:** the Hold and the city's legal framework are fictional. Real medication, therapy, ordinary grief, and clinical conditions must not be treated as interchangeable. Clinical staging and any production-facing legal language need specialist review before shooting.
